Aamir was born and raised in Oman, attending Indian School Al Wadi Al Kabir in Muscat until completing his 10th grade in 2021 (scoring 94.8% in CBSE).

In 2021, he moved to Bengaluru, Karnataka, India—the home city of his parents. He completed his 12th grade at Sindhi High School in Hebbal, Bengaluru (89.4% in CBSE, 2023), and is now pursuing his B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at M. S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences.

He appreciates managers and teammates who communicate context clearly, give direct feedback, and expect software to be thoroughly understood and tested. He views feedback as an opportunity to improve the work rather than a personal judgment, separating ego from implementation.

Aamir enjoys creative coding exercises outside his primary engineering focus, such as building 2D browser games with custom Canvas rendering, parallax camera logic, and sprite animation. These projects reflect his enthusiasm for understanding how software behaves at an interactive level.

In addition to software projects, he prioritizes physical fitness through bodyweight and resistance training. When encountering challenging technical roadblocks, his instinct is to isolate the problem into a minimal reproducible test case, consult logs and authoritative documentation, and verify each assumption systematically.
